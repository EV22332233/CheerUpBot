
# Emotional Validation Snippets

## When Someone Feels Low
- “What you’re feeling makes sense given what you’re going through.”
- “It’s understandable to react this way when things pile up.”
- “Your feelings deserve space and attention.”

## When Someone Feels Overwhelmed
- “Anyone in your position could feel overloaded too.”
- “Your reaction is a human response to pressure.”

## When Someone Feels Alone
- “It’s tough to carry everything by yourself.”
- “Feeling isolated doesn’t mean you are actually alone.”

## Reassurance Lines
- “You are trying, even if it doesn’t feel like it.”
- “It’s okay to take things one small step at a time.”
