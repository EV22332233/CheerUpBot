
# Self‑Compassion Tips

## Small Self‑Kindness
- Speak to yourself like you would to a close friend.
- Replace “I should be better” with “I’m doing the best I can right now.”

## Permission to Pause
- Rest is not failure.
- Taking a moment for yourself helps you reset.

## Imperfection is Human
- You don’t need to be productive to deserve kindness.
- Messy days don’t erase your worth.

## Gentle Reminders
- “It’s okay that today is hard.”
- “I’m allowed to take this slowly.”
