
# Safety & Crisis Guidance (Non‑Clinical)

## When You Feel Overwhelmed
- Pause and take one slow breath.
- Notice the physical sensations without judging them.
- It is okay to step away from the situation for a moment.

## If Emotions Feel Too Intense
- Reach out to someone you trust by message or call.
- Ground yourself using sensory Input: name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear.

## Seeking Real‑World Support
- If you ever feel unsafe, immediately contact your local emergency number.
- If you need to talk to someone urgently, reach out to national or local crisis hotlines in your region.
- Talking to a trained listener can provide relief and clarity.

## Reminders
- You are not a burden for asking for help.
- Intense moments do not define you.
- Support exists, and reaching for it is a strong and responsible choice.
