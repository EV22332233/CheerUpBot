
# Simple Gratitude Prompts

## Quick Prompts (30 Seconds)
- Name one thing today that didn’t go wrong.
- Recall one moment that made you exhale a little easier.
- Identify one person who made your life even 1% better.

## Reflective Prompts (1–3 Minutes)
- Think of a past challenge you handled better than expected.
- Recall a tiny sensory comfort you enjoy: warm light, soft fabric, a scent.
- Think of a compliment someone gave you once that stuck.

## “Future Gratitude”
- Imagine something small you look forward to tomorrow.
- Consider one supportive habit you want to thank yourself for building.
- Visualize a future version of you saying “thank you” to your present self for holding on.
