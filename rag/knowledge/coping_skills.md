
# Coping Skills – Quick, Gentle Techniques

## Breathing Reset
- Try slow 4‑second breaths: inhale 4, hold 2, exhale 6.
- Imagine the exhale releasing tension from shoulders and jaw.
- Repeat for 5 cycles.

## Small Physical Reset
- Roll shoulders backward 5 times.
- Unclench hands and stretch fingers.
- Look away from the screen toward something 3–5 meters away.

## Micro‑Break Routine
- Sit back and take a single deep breath.
- Relax your face muscles.
- Sip water slowly and mindfully.

## Environmental Comfort
- Dim harsh lighting if possible.
- Add a cozy object: blanket, hoodie, warm cup of tea.
- Put on gentle, familiar background sound.

## Emotional Cooling
- Label what you feel using simple words: “tired,” “overwhelmed,” “frustrated.”
- Remind yourself: feelings are temporary and will pass.

## Gentle Distraction Ideas
- Watch one short, uplifting video.
- Scroll through saved photos you enjoy.
- Play a calming song you know by heart.
