
# Grounding Exercises (Calming Techniques)

## 5‑4‑3‑2‑1 Method
- 5 things you can see  
- 4 things you can touch  
- 3 things you can hear  
- 2 things you can smell  
- 1 thing you can taste or imagine tasting

## Temperature Reset
- Hold something cool in your hands.
- Splash cold water on your face.
- Sit near fresh air if possible.

## Physical Anchor
- Press your feet into the floor gently.
- Notice the support of the chair beneath you.
- Wiggle your toes and notice the sensation.

## Thought Anchoring
- State your name and the date to yourself.
- Describe your surroundings like a narrator.
